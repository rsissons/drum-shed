How to use it

  1. Check where you are. The roadmap shows this block's rudiments and the order to work the rules in. Stay on the first rule until it moves around the kit without the time wobbling, then move to the next.
  2. Start the session timer. It runs all five blocks back to back and chimes when each one ends. Pick 30 min on short days; it runs blocks 2 to 4 only.
  3. Run the click from the bar at the bottom. Tap ▶ to start or stop, − and + to step the tempo. Tap the BPM for the tempo ladder: speed up 4 BPM after each clean pass, drop back 10 when it gets tense. Your fastest clean tempo is the number you log.
  4. Log the session when you finish. Save your top clean BPM and whether the groove + fill landed. Your log and gates are saved in this browser on this device only; nothing is sent anywhere.
  5. Pass the gate, move on. When you meet the gate, tap "Gate passed" and the next two-week block opens. Taking three weeks on a block is fine.
  6. Need the stickings? Tap 📋 Exercises for this block's patterns and the six orchestration rules. Tap ▶ next to any exercise to hear it and watch it move around a kit at your tempo, and 🎥 for a pro demo of the rudiment itself.

Rules are numbered easiest to hardest, so the numbers are the learning order. Each block uses a few of them, low to high.
