      How it works

      1. Pick your weeks on the first screen, then today's focus: one rudiment and the rule you're on. Stay on a rule until it moves around the kit without the time wobbling, then take the next one. Tap ▶ pad or ▶ kit next to any step to preview it.
      2. Hit Start. Practice mode shows one block at a time with a big clock. Tap ▶ Play along to follow the pad or kit and the count with the click, drums muted so you hear yourself. It moves on by itself with a chime. 30 min runs blocks 2 to 4 only.
      3. Run the click from the bar at the bottom. Tap the BPM for the tempo ladder: +4 after each clean pass, −10 when it gets tense.
      4. Log it when the session ends: your top clean BPM and whether the groove + fill landed. Your weeks and log are saved in this browser on this device only; nothing is sent anywhere.
      5. Move on when you can do the "Ready when" line: tap the weeks banner at the top and pick the next weeks. Taking three weeks on a block is fine.

      Rudiment videos are Vic Firth's free YouTube lessons by Dr. John Wooton (not affiliated). Orchestration ideas adapted from lessons on Mixdown and Drumeo. Free, no ads, no sign-up.
